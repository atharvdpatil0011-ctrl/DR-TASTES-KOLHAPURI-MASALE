Kolhapuri Tambada Rassa (तांबडा रस्सा)

The crown jewel of Kolhapuri mutton and chicken banquets. A thin, searingly aromatic crimson soup topped with a shimmering layer of spiced red oil.

35 minsPrep Time
4 ServingsYield
Spicy ★★★Heat Level
👑 Royal Secret: Never add extra garam masala or turmeric. DR Tastes Masala has the exact ratio of stone flower and roasted spices pre-balanced!

Simple 4-Step Method

  1. Boil Broth: Boil mutton or chicken with turmeric, salt, and ginger-garlic paste until tender to create a rich aromatic stock.
  2. Temper Aromatics: In a clay pot or heavy kadai, heat 3 tbsp oil. Add crushed onion paste and sauté until golden oil separates.
  3. The Magic Masala: Add 2 generous tablespoons of DR Tastes Kolhapuri Kanda Lasun Masala. Sauté on low flame for 60 seconds until the crimson fragrance fills your kitchen.
  4. Simmer & Serve: Pour in the strained boiling mutton stock. Let it roll to a furious boil for 5 minutes until the majestic red "Tarri" surfaces. Garnish with fresh coriander and serve boiling hot with Bhakri!
